What you can do with PowerSeva

One place for citizens, technicians and department officers.

Report with photos

Add up to five photos and your location so the crew can find the fault quickly.

Follow every step

See when your complaint is received, assigned, repaired and closed.

Get notified

You are told when a technician is assigned and when the work is finished.

Proof of repair

Technicians upload photos of the finished work, and you confirm that it is fixed.

Delays are escalated

If a repair takes longer than expected, it is passed to a senior officer automatically.

Reports for officers

Officers see response times, technician workload and citizen ratings in one place.
